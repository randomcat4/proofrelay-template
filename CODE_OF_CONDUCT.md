# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

Be precise, respectful, and evidence-focused. Critique claims and artifacts, not people. Report uncertainty and conflicts of interest. Do not pressure contributors to overstate results, hide negative evidence, reveal private data, or bypass review gates.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive, deceptive, privacy-violating, or security-sensitive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
